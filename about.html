Your Tariq Meat Shop, online.

Order online directly from Tariq Meat Shop, our physical shop in Al Aziziyah, Jeddah. Choose meat, poultry, eggs and vegetables, select your weights and cuts, and place your order on our website. We confirm your order and delivery availability on WhatsApp.

Our mission

We want to make everyday life easier, starting with the food you bring to your table. By bringing our local butcher shop online, we help you spend less time shopping and more time enjoying meals with the people who matter to you.

Why trust us?

We are a real neighbourhood shop in Al Aziziyah, Jeddah, with a team you can visit or contact directly. Our commitment is to provide quality meat, prepare your order with care, and make choosing your cuts and weights simple.

We believe our business grows when we give our customers real value. That means clear prices, helpful service, and listening to your feedback so we can keep improving. We want to earn your trust with every order and become a shop you feel confident returning to.
